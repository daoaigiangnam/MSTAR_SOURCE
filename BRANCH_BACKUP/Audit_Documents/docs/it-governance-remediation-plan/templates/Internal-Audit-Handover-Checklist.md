# Internal Audit Handover Checklist

- **Document ID:** TMP-AUDIT-HANDOVER-01
- **Version:** 0.1
- **Status:** Draft — pending UOA/Mstar review and approval
- **Effective date:** To be assigned after approval
- **Classification:** Internal / Restricted; share evidence only through approved channels

## 1. Purpose

Use this checklist to prepare an orderly, evidence-based handover to Internal Audit. It does not imply that Audit has accepted the evidence, agreed with management's position, or closed a finding.

## 2. Governance and scope

- [ ] Audit request, scope, period, criteria, and due dates are confirmed.
- [ ] Each issue is linked to a unique finding/CAPA ID.
- [ ] Statements are clearly labelled as reported, verified, partially verified, disputed, or pending validation.
- [ ] Process owners, control owners, action owners, and decision-makers are identified.
- [ ] Relevant process documents are version-controlled and their approval status is explicit.
- [ ] Unapproved drafts are not presented as approved policies or operating procedures.
- [ ] Open decisions, dependencies, exceptions, and overdue actions are disclosed.

## 3. Evidence quality and traceability

- [ ] Evidence register is current and every item has a unique evidence ID.
- [ ] Each item maps to a specific control, finding, CAPA action, or test criterion.
- [ ] Evidence records source, owner, collection date, period covered, and validation method.
- [ ] Screenshots, logs, exports, tickets, approvals, and test records are complete enough to reproduce the conclusion.
- [ ] Evidence is authentic, unaltered except for documented redaction, and retained under the approved policy.
- [ ] Time zones and timestamps are clear where relevant.
- [ ] Sampling population, selection method, sample size, and exceptions are documented.
- [ ] Negative evidence or failed tests are not omitted.
- [ ] Sensitive evidence is stored in an approved restricted repository; the handover contains evidence IDs/authorized links, not credentials or unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] Access to evidence is tested for the intended Audit recipients.

## 4. Access management and authorization evidence

- [ ] Access requests show requestor, business justification, target system/role, and scope.
- [ ] Required manager/data-owner/HR or other approvals are retained as required by the approved process.
- [ ] Provisioning/change/revocation timestamps and executor are traceable.
- [ ] Privileged and VPN access is specifically identified and reviewed.
- [ ] Exceptions, emergency grants, and retrospective records are clearly disclosed and handled under approved policy.
- [ ] Joiner/mover/leaver samples reconcile to HR and system records where authorized.
- [ ] No evidence is fabricated or backdated to fill missing approvals.

## 5. Backup, ownership, and recovery evidence

- [ ] Business/data owner and technical custodian are separately identified.
- [ ] Cloud tenant/account owner, contract party, billing owner, and access administrators are verified from authoritative records where relevant.
- [ ] Primary storage, replicas, backup destinations, retention, and access controls are documented.
- [ ] Business approval for backup location and cross-entity access is retained where required.
- [ ] Backup-job evidence is available for the requested period.
- [ ] Restore test records include integrity and business-usability validation.
- [ ] Approved RPO/RTO values are referenced; unapproved values are marked pending, not assumed.
- [ ] Unresolved ownership, contract, restore, or data-retention questions are listed as open actions.

## 6. CAPA and effectiveness

- [ ] Root-cause analysis distinguishes evidence from assumptions.
- [ ] Actions have accountable owners, due dates, dependencies, and measurable acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Implemented actions have supporting evidence.
- [ ] Effectiveness testing has a defined population, method, criteria, and result.
- [ ] Failed/partial tests and remaining risk are disclosed.
- [ ] Closure approval is recorded by the authorized role.
- [ ] Residual risk acceptance, if any, follows the approved exception process and is time-bound where appropriate.

## 8. Meeting protocol

- [ ] Assign one coordinator to maintain the request/evidence log.
- [ ] Answer the question asked; separate confirmed facts from interpretation.
- [ ] If an answer is unknown, say so, name the responsible owner, and provide a realistic follow-up date.
- [ ] Record Audit questions, evidence requested, commitments, owners, and due dates.
- [ ] Correct inaccurate statements promptly and preserve the correction trail.
- [ ] Do not promise a closure date until the responsible owner has validated feasibility.
- [ ] Obtain and circulate meeting notes through the approved channel.
