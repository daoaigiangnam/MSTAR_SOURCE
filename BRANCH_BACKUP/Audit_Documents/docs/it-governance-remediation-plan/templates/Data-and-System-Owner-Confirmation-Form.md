## 1. Purpose

Record business ownership and accountability for a data set, application, infrastructure service, backup copy, or cloud repository. Complete one form per system or clearly bounded data set. This form documents confirmation; it does not transfer legal ownership or replace contracts, privacy review, or formal approval.

## 3. Ownership and authority confirmation

The undersigned should confirm only matters within their authority.

- [ ] The business purpose and accountable business owner are identified.
- [ ] The data owner is authorized to determine permitted use and access, subject to applicable law and policy.
- [ ] The technical custodian is identified and is not automatically treated as the business owner.
- [ ] Approved users, roles, and access conditions are documented or linked.
- [ ] The primary storage and each known backup/replica location are recorded.
- [ ] Cloud/vendor account holder, tenant/subscription owner, and contract party have been verified from authoritative records.
- [ ] Data residency, cross-entity sharing, and third-party access have been assessed where applicable.
- [ ] Retention, recovery, export, and secure deletion requirements are documented or assigned for resolution.
- [ ] The backup location and access model are acceptable to the authorized business owner.
- [ ] Outstanding uncertainties and remediation actions are listed below.

Do not tick a box based solely on assumption, verbal recollection, or an unverified email. Mark it “Pending verification” and identify the evidence needed.
