## C. Decision definitions

- **Pass:** Evidence demonstrates that the control operated as required for the sample.
- **Fail:** Evidence demonstrates a deviation from a defined, applicable requirement.
- **Inconclusive:** The requirement, population, or evidence is insufficient to make a reliable decision.
- **Not applicable:** The control does not apply to this sample, with a documented rationale accepted by the reviewer.

Do not use “pass” based only on a procedure statement or an interview when operating evidence is required. Do not treat missing records as proof of intent.

## F. Evidence handling

Do not store passwords, tokens, private keys, full HR records, or unredacted production exports in Git. Store restricted evidence only in an approved repository with appropriate access controls, and reference it here by evidence ID. Preserve original timestamps and source metadata where permitted. Do not backdate approvals or reconstruct missing evidence as if it were contemporaneous.
