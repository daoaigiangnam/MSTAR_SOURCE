## 3. Minimum decision record

For each material decision, capture:
- Context and specific question
- Facts and evidence available at decision time
- Known uncertainty and alternatives
- Risk/impact of each option
- Recommended option and rationale
- Authority required by policy/delegation
- Actual decision, conditions and expiry/review date if applicable
- Decision-maker and date
- Related finding/CAPA/change/contract reference
- Communication and implementation owner

## 4. Escalation rules

- Escalate imminent or material access, data loss, exposure, or service continuity risks promptly through the approved channel.
- Escalate missing decisions that block a critical control or audit deadline with a documented impact and requested decision date.
- If a decision is not received, record “pending”; do not treat it as approval.
- If an exception or risk acceptance is approved, record its scope, compensating control, accountable owner, expiry/review date and approval evidence.
- If evidence is unavailable, record the limitation and the resulting assurance impact rather than inventing a record.
