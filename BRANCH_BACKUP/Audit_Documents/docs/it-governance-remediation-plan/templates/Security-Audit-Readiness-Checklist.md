## Audit interview preparation

- Assign one accountable owner per control area.
- Prepare a concise factual explanation of the process and show the corresponding evidence.
- If evidence is unavailable, state the gap, current risk and corrective action honestly.
- Do not reconstruct or backdate approvals.
- Keep original records intact; add corrective records with current dates.
- Separate reported concerns, verified findings and unresolved questions.
- Confirm evidence access permissions before sharing with auditors.
