5. Confirm backup data scope, account/storage ownership, data owner, retention and recovery objectives.
6. Agree evidence repository, classification, redaction, access permissions and authorized Audit handover channel.
7. Confirm control-test populations, sampling approach, reviewers and schedule.

10. Confirm actions, owners, due dates and next review.

## 5. Decisions requiring explicit approval

For each decision, record the approving authority, exact decision, date, conditions and reference:
- Audit scope, period and deadline
- Controlled procedures and approval matrix
- Evidence classification, storage, access and disclosure
- Interim control and remediation for confirmed high-risk conditions
- Backup target, recovery requirements, migration and separate deletion approval
- CAPA risk classification, risk acceptance and closure authority

Silence is not approval. If a decision is pending, record it as pending and document the impact of delay.
