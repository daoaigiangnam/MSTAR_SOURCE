## 1. Purpose

Ensure corrective and preventive actions are closed only after implementation is evidenced and effectiveness is tested. A published policy or completed training alone is not sufficient proof that a control operates effectively.

For access controls, sample at least relevant new grants, changes, privileged/VPN grants, and leaver revocations after the effective date. Confirm request → authorized approval → implementation → verification → register update. The actual sample size must be proportionate to risk and population; do not invent a fixed universal sample size.

For backup controls, verify an actual restore test against approved acceptance criteria and business/data owner confirmation; a successful backup-job log alone is insufficient to prove recovery.

## 6. Important restrictions

- Do not backdate approvals or alter original logs.
- Do not mark a finding closed because a document was drafted, uploaded or announced.
- Do not store passwords, private keys, tokens or raw sensitive production evidence in this repository.
- A risk acceptance decision is not equivalent to remediation; record it as risk accepted with owner, rationale, scope and expiry/review date.
