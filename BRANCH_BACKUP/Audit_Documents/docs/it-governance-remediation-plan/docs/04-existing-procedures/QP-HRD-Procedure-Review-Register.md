## Scope and limitation

This register covers QP-HRD-16, QP-HRD-17, QP-HRD-18, QP-HRD-22, QP-HRD-23 and QP-HRD-24. It is based on procedure titles and limited observations in the IT Governance Remediation Master Plan. It is not a completed line-by-line assessment of the original flowcharts. Obtain the current controlled copies, forms, process-owner interpretation and operating records before concluding that a step is missing or changing a procedure.

## Required review method

1. Obtain current controlled copy, version, owner and approval record.
2. Transcribe each source step and decision gate without silently rewriting it.
3. Review with the process owner and relevant stakeholders.
4. Trace a risk-based sample of real transactions to approval, execution evidence and register updates.
5. Classify issues as design ambiguity, documented-control gap, evidenced operating failure, evidence not located, or no issue identified.
6. Record rationale, owner, related forms/registers, implementation date and acceptance criteria for every proposed change.
7. Obtain formal approval before replacing a controlled procedure.
8. Test operation after implementation; publication alone does not establish effectiveness.
