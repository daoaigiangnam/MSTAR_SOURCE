# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Repository structure, Node.js API, SQLite jobs, health check, logging and tests.

## Phase 2 — Media ingestion
yt-dlp integration, metadata extraction, download jobs and FFmpeg utilities.

## Phase 3 — Speech pipeline
Audio extraction, Whisper transcription, SRT generation, sentence splitting and timing alignment.

## Phase 4 — Dubbing
Translation abstraction, TTS abstraction, voice configuration, synchronization and music preservation.

## Phase 5 — Rendering
Final audio/video composition, progress reporting, output management and cleanup/retry.

## Phase 6 — Production
Docker deployment, worker scaling, authentication, monitoring and CI/CD.
