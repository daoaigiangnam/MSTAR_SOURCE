## Rules
1. Every approved requirement receives a stable ID.
2. No requirement is considered complete without applicable verification evidence.
3. Missing links are tracked as gaps, not silently assumed complete.
4. IDs remain stable across document revisions where the requirement meaning is unchanged.
5. Changed requirements receive a documented change decision and impact assessment.
